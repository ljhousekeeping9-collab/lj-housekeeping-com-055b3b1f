# Luxe Cleaning Sites

Create a modern, luxury, professional website for LJ Housekeeping, using the uploaded LJ Housekeeping logo as the primary brand reference.

BRAND STYLE

Match the website closely to the logo:

Primary background: deep black / near-black

Accent color: electric/cool blue matching the logo

Secondary color: silver, white, and subtle metallic-gray

Clean, elegant typography

Premium luxury aesthetic

Minimal but visually impressive

Lots of clean spacing

Subtle blue glow effects and gradients

Smooth animations that feel professional, not flashy

High-end commercial/residential cleaning company feel

Avoid bright colors, clutter, cartoon graphics, or generic cleaning-company templates

The website should feel like a company that provides premium, reliable, detail-focused cleaning services.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Create only these main pages:

HOME

ABOUT US

REQUEST AN ESTIMATE

Do NOT create an online booking system, calendar, shopping cart, or payment page. The main purpose of the website is to make LJ Housekeeping look professional and make it extremely easy for potential clients to request an estimate.



HOME PAGE

Create a strong luxury hero section.

Use the LJ Housekeeping logo prominently.

Headline:

YOUR SPACE. OUR PRIORITY.

Subheadline:

Professional cleaning services designed to keep your home or business looking its best.

Primary button:

REQUEST AN ESTIMATE

Secondary button:

LEARN ABOUT US

The primary CTA should take visitors directly to the Request an Estimate page.

Add a subtle premium background image or dark visual of a beautifully maintained home/business, with a dark overlay so the blue and silver branding remains dominant.

HOME PAGE SECTIONS

WHY LJ HOUSEKEEPING

Create 3–4 elegant feature cards:

Professional Service
Reliable cleaning with attention to detail.

Consistent Results
We focus on maintaining a clean, polished environment every time.

Detail Focused
We don’t just clean — we pay attention to the details that make a space feel truly refreshed.

Built Around You
Cleaning services tailored to your home’s or business’s needs.

Keep these cards minimal with subtle blue/silver icons.

OUR SERVICES

Show a clean overview of services without making the page overly long.

Include:

Residential Cleaning
Professional cleaning designed to keep your home fresh, comfortable, and presentable.

Commercial Cleaning
Reliable cleaning solutions for offices, salons, businesses, and professional spaces.

Deep Cleaning
Detailed cleaning for spaces that need extra attention and a more thorough reset.

Add a small button:

REQUEST AN ESTIMATE

Do not display fixed prices. Every estimate should be customized.

A PREMIUM DIFFERENCE

Create a visually striking section with a large statement:

“A cleaner space. A better experience.”

Explain that LJ Housekeeping focuses on professionalism, consistency, attention to detail, and making clients feel confident in their space.

FINAL CALL TO ACTION

End the homepage with a large, elegant section:

READY FOR A CLEANER SPACE?

Tell us about your space and let us create a personalized estimate for you.

Button:

REQUEST AN ESTIMATE



ABOUT US PAGE

Make this page stand out and feel more personal than a typical “About Us” page.

Start with a dramatic dark hero section:

ABOUT LJ HOUSEKEEPING

Subheadline:

Professional cleaning. Personal attention.

Use a large premium image of a spotless, modern home or professional business environment.

OUR STORY

Create a section explaining that LJ Housekeeping was created with a simple goal: to provide dependable, professional cleaning services while giving every client the level of care and attention they deserve.

Keep the writing genuine and confident — not overly corporate.

Emphasize:

Professionalism

Reliability

Attention to detail

Consistency

Respect for clients’ spaces

Pride in the work

OUR MISSION

Create a visually distinct section with a blue glow/accent:

OUR MISSION

“To provide dependable, high-quality cleaning services that allow our clients to enjoy a cleaner, more comfortable, and more professional space without having to worry about the details.”

WHY WE DO IT

Create a more emotional section explaining that a clean environment can change how a space feels and that LJ Housekeeping wants clients to have one less thing to worry about.

Use a large quote-style design:

“We take care of the cleaning, so you can focus on what matters most.”

OUR STANDARD

Create 4 premium visual points:

QUALITY
We take pride in doing the job right.

RELIABILITY
Our clients should be able to count on us.

DETAIL
The little things make a big difference.

PROFESSIONALISM
We treat every space with care and respect.

End the About page with:

LET US TAKE CARE OF THE CLEANING.

Button:

REQUEST AN ESTIMATE

Keep it extremely clean, simple, and professional.

Subheadline:

Tell us a little about your space and what you’re looking for. We’ll review your request and get back to you with your estimate.

How did you hear about us?

Submit button:

Do not show pricing automatically. The purpose of this form is to collect information so LJ Housekeeping can provide a personalized estimate.



NAVIGATION

Keep the navigation extremely simple:

HOME | ABOUT US | REQUEST AN ESTIMATE

Place a prominent REQUEST AN ESTIMATE button on the right side of the navigation.

Make the navigation sticky when scrolling.

On mobile, use a clean hamburger menu.



FOOTER

Create a premium dark footer using the LJ Housekeeping logo.

Include:

LJ HOUSEKEEPING

Your Home, Our Priority.

Navigation:
Home | About Us | Request an Estimate

Include social media icons if social media links are provided.

Add:

© 2026 LJ Housekeeping. All Rights Reserved.



DESIGN DETAILS

The website should feel similar to a luxury service brand, not a cheap cleaning template.

Use:

Black backgrounds

White typography

Blue accent lighting

Silver details

Thin elegant borders

Subtle shadows

Soft gradients

Smooth hover effects

Professional photography

Rounded corners, but keep them subtle

Modern premium typography

Avoid:

Excessive animations

Too much text

Bright green cleaning imagery

Cartoon cleaning icons

Stock-photo-heavy layouts

Huge paragraphs

Cheap-looking buttons

Overcrowded sections

Pricing tables

The website should be fast, mobile-friendly, easy to navigate, and visually impressive.

The overall feeling should be:

Luxury • Professional • Reliable • Clean • Modern • Trustworthy

The primary goal of the entire website is to turn visitors into estimate requests.

Every major section should naturally lead the visitor toward:

REQUEST AN ESTIMATE
